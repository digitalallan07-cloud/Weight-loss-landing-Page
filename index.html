Our Services

VLCC Weight Management & Slimming

VLCC Weight Management & Slimming is a programme recommended by IMA that promotes advanced weight management and healthy living using minimally invasive to completely non-invasive treatments. It focuses on helping customers achieve the right body weight and shape while controlling, reducing, and removing excess body weight and unwanted fat.

VLCC CoolSculpting

A non-surgical body contouring procedure, CoolSculpting is a highly advanced service that enables fat reduction from any or all parts of the body. It is an FDA-approved treatment that offers permanent fat loss treatment, resulting in a visibly slimmer and contoured body shape for figure enhancement.

VLCC Body Therapies

VLCC body therapies include a range of special programs to restore good health. Conducted by Experts, VLCC body therapy sessions involve high technology machinery to break down stubborn fat, reduce stress, improve physical health, cure body pains and much more.

Aesthetic Dermatology

VLCC aesthetic dermatology aims at successfully improving physical features and skin quality to keep you looking beautiful, healthy and radiant. The program uses the latest technology to provide effective treatment for a variety of skin conditions like acne, pigmentation, dark spots, scars, cellulite marks, signs of ageing and much more.

Beauty Services

VLCC Grooming & Regular Beauty services comprises all essential services that elevates the physical appearance of individuals. VLCC Beauty services include a range of treatments – basic to high-end, like threading, facials, clean ups, massages and much more to keep you looking gorgeous and ready, everyday.

Hair Technology

VLCC Hair & Hair technology involves grooming methods curated especially to augment hair health. From haircuts to hair care treatments like spas and oil therapies, the service comprises essentials that aim to improve hair texture, appearance, and growth while combating stress, environmental aggression, and pollution.

VLCC Laser Treatments

VLCC laser treatments involve the usage of the latest technology to ensure safe and complete reduction of unwanted body hair. The service aims at delivering permanent results while keeping the skin safe to ensure smooth, soft and even toned skin for all complexions using non-invasive and non-surgical treatments.
